### 🎯 Executive Summary & Market Fit

#### The Problem
Traditional sports drills are highly repetitive, and private coaching is expensive. Without a structured plan, players often drop into "Open Play" sessions and resort to mindless hitting, which reinforces bad habits instead of building actual skills.

#### The Solution
This app injects deliberate, intentional goals into casual play, turning physical conditioning into an addictive digital card game. <br> It is strategically built for:
* **Fundamentally-Sound Beginners:** Players who know the rules but need structure to transition from basic hitting to strategic play.
* **Post-Coach Graduates:** Players who took a few fundamental lessons but now need an affordable, independent way to sharpen their skills.
* **Ambitious Intermediates:** Players looking to break through training plateaus and elevate their competitive tier.
* **Racket Sport Veterans:** Tennis or badminton players who understand ball-striking but need to master pickleball-specific positioning quickly without the expense of a coach.
